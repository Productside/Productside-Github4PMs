## "But we already have..."

You do. Keep using those tools for what they are good at. The question is what they cannot do.

&nbsp;

**SharePoint / Google Drive**
- Does well: Store, organize, and govern finalized documents
- Cannot do: Show you a line-level diff of what changed between two versions of a strategy document, who changed it, and why. No branching. No concurrent editing without "FINAL_v7" naming.

**Confluence**
- Does well: Publish and navigate shared knowledge
- Cannot do: Let three people explore competing interpretations of new evidence on separate branches and compare the exact differences. Page history is snapshots, not a reviewable trail of reasoning.

**Claude Projects / ChatGPT Projects**
- Does well: Give one person persistent AI context
- Cannot do: Share that context across a team. Your AI session is yours. A colleague's is theirs. Nobody sees what the other changed, and neither AI reads what the other produced.

**Slack / Teams**
- Does well: Fast, informal conversation
- Cannot do: Preserve decisions. The answer to "when did we decide to change the positioning?" is never "scroll up."

&nbsp;

**GitHub is not replacing any of these.** It is the layer underneath for product context that is actively evolving, contested, and needs to be traceable.
