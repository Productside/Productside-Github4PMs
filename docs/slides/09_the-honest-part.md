### 01 · The UI curve

GitHub's interface was designed for developers. Your team will hit moments where something is harder than it should be. That is real, and it is not a reason to stop.

### 02 · No Gantt charts

GitHub is not a project management tool. It does not replace Jira, Asana, or Linear for sprint tracking and scheduling. It replaces the place where your thinking lives and compounds.

### 03 · Not everything belongs here

Financial models, customer PII, legal contracts, board decks. Some material has access requirements that a GitHub repo cannot enforce well enough. Know what goes in and what stays out.

&nbsp;

> **"Your strategy doc gets rewritten every Monday. Your repo doesn't have to."**
