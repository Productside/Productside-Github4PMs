## Workflow 8 · Dean merges. The library improves.

&nbsp;

**The Situation:**
Dean reviewed Kenny's pull request. The content guard passed. The changes are sound. Dean approves and merges. Kenny's battle card improvements are now part of the published library.

**The PM Gain:**
Tribal knowledge becomes team infrastructure. Kenny's field experience with battle cards is now baked into a skill anyone can run. The next PM who runs the Battle Card Builder gets Kenny's improvements without knowing Kenny.
