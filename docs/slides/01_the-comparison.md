&nbsp;

These are not bad tools. They are built for different kinds of work. The question is what happens when your product knowledge needs to evolve collaboratively over time.

| | **Optimized for** | **Where it falls short for evolving product knowledge** |
|---|---|---|
| **SharePoint / Drive** | Storing and sharing files | No required explanation for changes. Conversations detached from files. Linear version history. |
| **Confluence** | Static reference pages | Divorced from work orchestration. Cannot attach a discussion to the paragraph that sparked it. |
| **Jira** | Delivery mechanics | Drags every fleeting idea into sprints, story points, and backlog tickets. Too heavy for discovery. |
| **Claude / AI projects** | Isolated AI exploration | Context trapped inside individual sessions. No shared history, no review, no branching. |
| **GitHub** | Collaborative, evolving, structured work | Designed for developers. The UI has a learning curve. Not a project management tool. |

&nbsp;

This is not "move everything to GitHub." Your slide decks, budgets, and contracts stay where they are.

The claim is narrower: **your team's strategy, decisions, and research belong somewhere they can evolve together, be reviewed, and be reused.** A file share stores. A repo compounds.
