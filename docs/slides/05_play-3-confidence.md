## Evidence

From assumption to decision, connected. The one most teams cannot do: a finding that says "we were wrong" is only valuable if it is findable later.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  AS["Assumption<br/>we believe X"]
  EX["Experiment<br/>here is how<br/>we would know"]
  EV["Evidence<br/>cited, dated"]
  DE{"Were we<br/>right"}
  KE["Decision recorded<br/>and acted on"]
  WR["Wrong, recorded<br/>as such"]

  AS --> EX --> EV --> DE
  DE -->|"yes"| KE
  DE -->|"no"| WR
  WR -.->|"sharpens the<br/>next assumption"| AS
```
