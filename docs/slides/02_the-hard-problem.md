## The hard problem is not creating another document.

It is keeping the product team oriented while the context changes. Research challenges assumptions. Strategy moves. Decisions get revisited. AI generates alternatives.

&nbsp;

| What goes wrong | What should happen instead |
|---|---|
| FINAL_v7.docx, mysterious edits, lost history | What changed? Why? When? Who changed it? |
| Scattered across Notion, Slack, Drive, email | What alternatives did we reject? |
| Every AI session starts from zero | Can I explore something new without stepping on everyone else's work? |
