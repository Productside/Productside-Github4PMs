## Workflow 7 · Give AI the same context as the team

&nbsp;

**The Situation:**
Every AI session starts from zero because your context lives in your head, not in files the AI can read. When Kenny cloned the MI Skills repo and ran a skill, the AI read the repository's prompts, reference frameworks, evidence standards, and guardrails. It did not hallucinate them.

**The PM Gain:**
The team and its AI systems work from the same persistent context. The AI reads the repo, not your clipboard. The guardrails, the evidence labels, the do-not-invent list all travel with the skill.

&nbsp;

> **The AI Prompt**
>
> ```
> Read this repository and tell me what skills are available,
> what frameworks they use, and what rules govern how evidence
> gets labeled. Use the actual files, not your training data.
> ```
>
> ---
>
> **The Invisible Machinery**
>
> Claude Code reads `CONSTITUTION.md`, `reference/`, `prompts/`, and `CLAUDE.md`. The context survives the session because it lives in files, not in a chat window that disappears.

&nbsp;

**The difference:**
- **Session-based AI** forgets everything when the window closes
- **Repo-based AI** reads what the team wrote down: evidence standards, frameworks, guardrails, the do-not-invent list
- The context survives the session, the reorg, and the person who wrote it leaving
