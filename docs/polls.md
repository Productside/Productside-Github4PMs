# Audience Polls

**Source of truth for both polls.** `run-of-show.md` and `slide-plan.md` point here — change the wording in this file only.

Productside webinars run two polls: one after the agenda to set up the topic, one at the close asking what the audience will do differently. Both use the `w-poll` layout (one question, options `01`–`05`).

**They share a single taxonomy: the five plays.** Poll 1 names five problems before the agenda names five answers. Poll 2 asks the same five back as commitments. The delta between them is the signal.

| | Poll 1 | Poll 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Slide | 8, after the agenda | after the close hero |
| Runs at | 0:08–0:10 | 0:52–0:54 |
| Open for | ~60 seconds | ~60 seconds |
| Read by | Kenny | Kenny |

---

## Poll 1 · Setup

**Question:**

> Where does your product team lose the most time?

**Options:**

| | Option | Play |
|---|---|---|
| `01` | Rebuilding context that already existed | Context |
| `02` | Relitigating decisions nobody wrote down | Communications |
| `03` | Redoing research nobody can find | Confidence |
| `04` | Chasing people to follow the rules | Constitution |
| `05` | Answering the same request in five places | Community |

**How to run it.** Launch it right after the agenda, leave it open about a minute, then read the results out loud and **name the play that covers the winner**. That single sentence is what turns the agenda from a feature list into a list of answers.

**The live steer:** if one option runs away with it, spend an extra minute on that play and take the minute from one that landed low. Decide that in the moment — the plays are written to survive it, except Play 5, which is the ending and does not get shaved.

---

## Poll 2 · Commitment

**Question:**

> Starting today, what will you do differently?

**Options:**

| | Option | Play |
|---|---|---|
| `01` | Give strategy and decisions one versioned home | Context |
| `02` | Move the argument somewhere it survives | Communications |
| `03` | Connect assumptions to the evidence behind them | Confidence |
| `04` | Write the guardrails down | Constitution |
| `05` | Build a real front door for requests | Community |

**How to run it.** Launch it after the closing hero slide, before Q&A. **Compare the results against Poll 1 out loud.** Saying the comparison in the room is what makes the audience feel the arc close, and it costs fifteen seconds.

**Why it earns its slot.** It doubles as a recap without a recap slide, it converts watching into a commitment, and the Poll 1 → Poll 2 delta tells you which play actually moved someone. That comparison is the most useful thing this session produces for Productside besides the registrations — worth capturing from the platform afterward rather than losing it in the recording.

---

## For whoever loads these into the platform

- Two polls, one question each, five options each. Single choice, not multi-select — the whole point is forcing a priority.
- No "other" option and no free text. Five categories is the taxonomy the session teaches.
- Results need to be visible to the presenters in the moment, not just exported afterward.
- **Export both result sets after the session.** The comparison is the deliverable.
